15 Feb 2026
Poster Presentation

Air Quality Monitoring Using Federated Learning Under Heterogeneous Resource Constraints

Department of Environmental Science and Engineering
International Workshop, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, India

Air quality monitoring systems use many distributed sensors to collect environmental data and images. These sensors often differ in hardware capability, network quality, and data availability. Designing learning systems that can work reliably across such heterogeneous devices is challenging. Federated Learning enables distributed model training, but effective architectures for multimodal air quality data are still not well studied. The poster can be found here.

Presentation

Introduction to Federated Learning [PDF]

Mathematics Association of Cambodia (MAC)
Online Seminar, Dhanbad, India

This webinar introduces Federated Learning (FL), a centralized approach that keeps data local while training models collaboratively. We explore FL workflow, client-server optimization techniques, and challenges like data heterogeneity. Using CIFAR-10 with the Flower framework, we present results, applications, and future directions. The poster can be found here.
